## About SableOS 2.0

SableOS is a BlackBerry-style Android for the Zinwa Q25. SableOS 2.0 moves to
LineageOS 24.0 (Android 17) and adds a new Phone, a Hub that sorts what
matters, and many BlackBerry 10 style touches to the home screen, lock screen
and setup. The keyboard is still the centre of the phone: almost everything can
be driven without touching the screen.

Everything in [SableOS 1.0](1.0.md) is still here except microG, Aurora Store and
Sable Mail (see "Removed" below). This page lists what changed.

## What's new in 2.0

### Home screen (Sable Start)

- **Hub**: Priority Hub puts people you reply to first; each message can be
  snoozed or set to remind you later; Upcoming shows the next events; Mark
  prior read clears older messages up to the one you pick (never all at once).
  Hold a row for the BlackBerry 10 style row menu. Swipe right on Hub to open
  Hub settings.
- **Active Frames**: live media controls, and a Device Monitor frame (battery,
  CPU, memory and storage) while Settings is open. Frames have their own
  options.
- **Calendar**: busy rings on each day and a Month view.
- **Command**: instant actions as you type, prefixes, and Alt+Space opens
  Command from any app.
- **App grid**: folders, a choice of grid shape, hidden apps, your own tabs and
  hold-a-letter jumps. The bar and page dots match the BlackBerry 10 style.
- **Trackpad flick** between pages, BlackBerry 7 style Delete on the home
  screen, and a new tray layout.
- **One app for each job**: the home no longer carries its own Phone, Calendar,
  Calculator, Notes, sound profiles or games, so calls always go to Sable Phone
  and the ringer button opens Sable Profiles. The home never asks to record the
  screen, and its own setup no longer opens after SableOS setup.
- **Balance** and **Sable Spaces**: a separate Work space with its own options.

### Lock screen and setup

- **Peek** shows the time, next event and notifications before you unlock.
- A compact PIN pad and compact notification rows that fit the square screen.
- **Picture Password**: unlock by moving a number to a spot on your picture.
- **Sable Setup** asks for text size, offers Full, Essentials or Stock app
  sets, and has a Look step for the home style and theme.

### Phone and messages

- **Sable Phone**, a new keyboard-first dialer: letter-key contact search,
  hardware digits, speed dial, call log with missed-call clearing, and dual-SIM
  account choice.

### New apps and features

- **Sable Speech** (eSpeak NG text-to-speech), and Text Reader can speak the
  focused item.
- **Sable Tasks**, **Bedside mode**, **Sable Browser**, **Print To Go** and
  **Compass**.
- **The system menu**, as on BlackBerry 7: Alt+BlackBerry key in any app
  opens the app's menu, Close, Switch Application, Notifications and Quick
  Settings, all with the trackpad. Quick Settings is now a one-column list.
- **Cosgrove**, an assistant you hold a key to talk to. It never listens on
  its own. Hold the BlackBerry key to open him over any app. He can text someone for you: type
  `text Anna: running late`, or ask him, and he shows the text to you first;
  nothing is sent until you press Send.
- **Weather alerts**, off until you turn them on: a morning forecast and a
  warning when rain is coming in the next 3 hours.

### Settings and system

- **Deep sleeping apps** (was "Put unused apps to sleep"): apps you have not
  opened for a while can be put to sleep automatically, with a keep-awake list.
  Sable apps, accessibility services, device admins and the live wallpaper are
  never slept.
- New Sable Settings pages and a trackpad mode setting.
- **USB drives** plug in through the USB-C port (USB 2.0) and open in Files.
- Fixed: phantom key presses right after the screen wakes.
- The Sable platform apps that were written in Rust are now Kotlin.

## Removed

- **No Google software and no app store in the image.** microG and Aurora Store
  are no longer built in. If you need apps that use Google Play services,
  install the microG add-on from a computer after setup with one command; it
  installs microG Services, Companion, Aurora Store and the Services Framework
  Proxy, which microG and Aurora need. Aurora Store works with its anonymous sign-in;
  signing in with your own Google account does not work yet. See
  [MICROG.md](https://github.com/aimindseye/zinwaq25-sableos/blob/main/docs/MICROG.md).
- **Sable Mail is not installed.** Hub reads any mail app's notifications, so
  K-9 Mail, FairEmail or Gmail fill the Hub's Mail filter.

## Known issues

- Features marked "not run on device" in the
  [qualification list](https://github.com/aimindseye/zinwaq25-sableos/blob/main/docs/QUALIFICATION.md)
  may not work yet.
- The Q25 has no wired video out: its USB-C port does not carry DisplayPort.
  Use the 3.5 mm jack for wired audio.
- The keyboard backlight stays off with the stock keyboard firmware.
- Google sign-in through the microG add-on is still being checked; some apps
  that need Google Play services may not work.
- Updating from 1.0 (Android 16) to 2.0 (Android 17) keeps data only if the
  update is qualified; back up and expect a clean install.
- Sable app translations are machine-assisted drafts.
