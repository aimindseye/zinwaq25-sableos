## About SableOS 2.0

SableOS is a BlackBerry-style Android for the Zinwa Q25. SableOS 2.0 moves to
LineageOS 24.0 (Android 17) and adds a new Phone, a Hub that sorts what
matters, and many BlackBerry 10 style touches to the home screen, lock screen
and setup. The keyboard is still the centre of the phone: almost everything can
be driven without touching the screen.

Everything in [SableOS 1.0](1.0.md) is still here except microG and Aurora Store
(see "Removed" below). This page lists what changed.

## What's new in 2.0

### Home screen (Sable Start)

- **A new Sable Start**, rebuilt on TenSeven Launcher and following the
  approved BlackBerry 10 design. It keeps the same name and shortcuts.
- **Hub**: Priority Hub puts people you reply to first; each message can be
  snoozed or set to remind you later; Upcoming shows the next events; Mark
  prior read clears older messages up to the one you pick (never all at once).
  Hold a row for the BlackBerry 10 style row menu. Swipe right on Hub to open
  Hub settings.
- **Hub keeps your messages** until you delete them, on the phone only, even
  after the app clears its notification. It lists Unread first, then Today,
  Yesterday and earlier days. Views lists each app with its unread count and
  Priority Hub; the ⋮ menu opens Hub settings, Email accounts and Calendar
  accounts. The row menu adds Mark unread and Delete. Hub settings can show
  every message on its own and leave out Android's system notices.
- **Active Frames** show each app's live picture, as in 1.0, with a slim name
  bar; media and message summaries show only when there is no picture.
- **Active Frames**: live media controls, and a Device Monitor frame (battery,
  CPU, memory and storage) while Settings is open. Frames have their own
  options.
- **Calendar**: busy rings on each day and a Month view.
- **Command**: instant actions as you type, prefixes, and Alt+Space opens
  Command from any app.
- **App grid**: folders, a choice of grid shape, hidden apps, your own tabs and
  hold-a-letter jumps. The bar and page dots match the BlackBerry 10 style.
- **Trackpad flick** between pages, BlackBerry 7 style Delete on the home
  screen, and a new tray layout.
- **One app for each job**: the home no longer carries its own Phone, Calendar,
  Calculator, Notes, sound profiles or games, so calls always go to Sable Phone
  and the ringer button opens Sable Profiles. The home never asks to record the
  screen, and its own setup no longer opens after SableOS setup.
- **Balance** and **Sable Spaces**: a separate Work space with its own options.

### Lock screen and setup

- **Peek** shows the time, next event and notifications before you unlock.
  With no notifications, the lock layer is just the clock and date.
- A compact PIN pad and compact notification rows that fit the square screen.
- **Picture Password**: unlock by moving a number to a spot on your picture.
- **Wipe after 10 failed tries** (off by default): erase the phone after ten
  wrong PINs or passwords in a row, with warnings before the last tries.
- **Sable Setup** asks for text size, offers Full, Essentials or Stock app
  sets, and has a Look step for the home style and theme.

### Phone and messages

- **Sable Phone**, a new keyboard-first dialer: letter-key contact search,
  hardware digits, speed dial, call log with missed-call clearing, and dual-SIM
  account choice. Calls and contacts show the contact's photo, or initials.

### New apps and features

- **Email in the Hub**, as on BlackBerry 10: add your Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo,
  iCloud or work account in **Sable Mail**, and the Hub lists your inboxes
  with the rest of your messages. New mail arrives by push (IMAP IDLE), with no
  Gmail or Outlook app. Open a message from the Hub and it opens in Sable Mail;
  mark it read in the Hub and it is read in Sable Mail and on the server too.
  Gmail and Outlook sign-in need the build's own sign-in IDs
  ([Gmail](https://github.com/aimindseye/zinwaq25-sableos/blob/main/docs/MAIL_GMAIL_SIGNIN.md), [Outlook](https://github.com/aimindseye/zinwaq25-sableos/blob/main/docs/MAIL_MICROSOFT_SIGNIN.md));
  other accounts sign in with a password or app password.
- **Sable Calendar** shows every calendar: each day's ring is split by
  calendar colour, and there is a new Day view. **Add account** takes Google,
  iCloud and Yahoo calendars through **DAVx⁵**, and an Outlook calendar link
  through **ICSx⁵**; both are in the image, in Sable's black, white and blue.
- **Sable Speech** (eSpeak NG text-to-speech), and Text Reader can speak the
  focused item.
- **Sable Tasks**, **Bedside mode**, **Sable Browser**, **Print To Go** and
  **Compass**.
- **The system menu**, as on BlackBerry 7: Alt+BlackBerry key in any app
  opens the app's menu, Close, Switch Application, Notifications and Quick
  Settings, all with the trackpad. Quick Settings is now a one-column list.
- **Cosgrove**, an assistant you hold a key to talk to. It never listens on
  its own. Hold the BlackBerry key to open him over any app. He can text someone for you: type
  `text Anna: running late`, or ask him, and he shows the text to you first;
  nothing is sent until you press Send.
- **Weather alerts**, off until you turn them on: a morning forecast and a
  warning when rain is coming in the next 3 hours.
- **Sable Games Console**: games written as small Lua scripts that you can
  add from a folder without a new build. It comes with eight games you play by
  tilting the phone (Tilt Maze, Motion 2048, Sokoban Tilt, Tilt Racer with F1
  cars in the colour you choose, Tilt Circuit, three laps against two
  rivals on an easy oval or a twisty circuit, and Ball Maze, steel balls in a round or square maze of plastic or
  wood, Sky Roll, a ball rolled over platforms floating in the sky, and Ramp
  Rush, a stunt car racing and jumping through a desert canyon), and they play
  with the keys too. Games can bring their own pictures. Hold the phone how you like and that becomes
  level. Games run in a sandbox with no access to your files or the network; loops, runaway text searches and memory floods stop the game, not the phone.
- **Sable Media** on the square screen: a Hub-style list with one row of
  tabs, cover art, podcast covers and radio station logos, and your own names
  for FM stations.
- **Sable Browser** pages follow the light or dark theme.

### Settings and system

- **App Compatibility** (was App display compatibility) gives each app a
  layout: Square (the native 720×720), Tall phone (720×1440 with side bars) or
  Custom. **App size** shows one app at 100%, 85% or 75%, so more fits on the
  screen, only while that app is in front. **Landscape video Fit** gives a Tall
  phone app the full square while it plays sideways, so a 16:9 video fills the
  width. YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook start as Tall phone, Google
  Maps and Android Auto as Square; a new app gets its preset when it is
  installed, and Reset brings the preset back.
- **Pluto TV opens again.** Android 14's rule against loading code an app can
  still change made its ad library close it at start. Apps on App
  Compatibility's **Older code loading** list (Pluto TV by default) may load it;
  every other app keeps the rule.

- **Deep sleeping apps** (was "Put unused apps to sleep"): apps you have not
  opened for a while can be put to sleep automatically, with a keep-awake list.
  Sable apps, accessibility services, device admins and the live wallpaper are
  never slept.
- New Sable Settings pages and a trackpad mode setting.
- **Sable Audio** (Connected devices): pick the Bluetooth codec, sample rate,
  bit depth and LDAC quality for your headphones, and HD audio for each one.
- **Key remapping** (Keyboard): the $ key, right Shift or Recents key can be
  Ctrl, Alt, Tab, Esc, Menu or Search in every app.
- **Space, T and B** page and jump to the top or bottom in other apps too,
  wherever the app does not use the key itself.
- **Back** at an app's first screen shows Active Frames, as on BlackBerry 10.
- **Hub over any app**: hold the Recents key, or choose Hub in the
  Alt+BlackBerry key menu, to see your newest messages over the app you are
  in. Back returns to it.
- **Frame covers**: apps can say what their own Active Frame shows.
- **No setup for the home**: Contacts, calendar, photos, notifications,
  notification access, usage access and display over other apps are granted
  to the home on its first start, so Hub, Active Frames and media frames work
  at once. Launcher settings no longer has an Active Frames page.
- Fixed: the lock screen PIN pad could hide its bottom row under the
  navigation bar.
- **Dimmer at night**: with the brightness slider at the bottom, Extra dim
  turns on, so the screen goes dimmer than the panel's lowest backlight step.
  It turns off when you move the slider up.
- **USB drives** plug in through the USB-C port (USB 2.0) and open in Files.
- **Key Mapper** is included: remap any Q25 key, or give a key a long-press
  or double-press shortcut.
- **Every app's keys** are listed in one place: docs/KEYBOARD_SHORTCUTS.md.
  The User Guide now gives Mail's, Camera's and Media's real keys.
- Fixed: phantom key presses right after the screen wakes.
- Fixed: letters typing numbers in every app. The keyboard driver had a
  hidden number lock (Left Alt + Right Shift); it is gone.
- Fixed: Calendar and other Sable apps drawing under the status bar.
- **Privacy Screen darkness**: Dim, Dark or Black.
- Every Sable app uses the Sable font at Sable sizes, a little smaller on
  the Q25.
- The Sable platform apps that were written in Rust are now Kotlin.

## Removed

- **No Google software and no app store in the image.** microG and Aurora Store
  are no longer built in. If you need apps that use Google Play services,
  install the microG add-on from a computer after setup with one command; it
  installs microG Services, Companion, Aurora Store and the Services Framework
  Proxy, which microG and Aurora need. Aurora Store works with its anonymous sign-in;
  signing in with your own Google account does not work yet. See
  [MICROG.md](https://github.com/aimindseye/zinwaq25-sableos/blob/main/docs/MICROG.md).

## Known issues

- Features marked "not run on device" in the
  [qualification list](https://github.com/aimindseye/zinwaq25-sableos/blob/main/docs/QUALIFICATION.md)
  may not work yet.
- The Q25 has no wired video out: its USB-C port does not carry DisplayPort.
  Use the 3.5 mm jack for wired audio.
- The keyboard backlight stays off with the stock keyboard firmware.
- Google sign-in through the microG add-on is still being checked; some apps
  that need Google Play services may not work.
- Updating from 1.0 (Android 16) to 2.0 (Android 17) keeps data only if the
  update is qualified; back up and expect a clean install.
- Sable app translations are machine-assisted drafts.
