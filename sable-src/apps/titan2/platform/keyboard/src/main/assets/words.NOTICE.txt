words_<lang>.tsv and emoji_en.tsv: notices
==========================================

Both are data generated by sable-src/tools/gen_word_lists.py from the inputs below. They power the keyboard's
suggestion strip (autocomplete, spelling suggestions, emoji) and the system spell checker. Nothing the user
types is added to them or stored.
