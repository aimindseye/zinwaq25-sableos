pinyin_zh.tsv: notices
======================

pinyin_zh.tsv is data generated by tools/gen_pinyin_dict.py from exactly two input datasets. The generator
is offline and deterministic; the inputs are pinned by sha256 in third_party/pinyin_zh/PROVENANCE.env.
The full licence texts are in pinyin_zh.LICENSES.txt, shipped alongside this file.

Modifications by Sable: the data was changed from the upstream form. The generator selects the Simplified
Chinese form of each CC-CEDICT entry, filters entries (Han characters only, one to six characters, valid
pinyin only), normalizes pinyin (lowercase, ue-diaeresis written v, circumflex dropped), strips tones to
form the lookup key, removes syllable separators, deduplicates readings, merges the admitted Unihan
readings for single characters, assigns deterministic ranking weights, and sorts the result into the Sable
runtime TSV format (pinyin<TAB>word<TAB>weight). The weight is a neutral ranking weight and is NOT a word
frequency.
